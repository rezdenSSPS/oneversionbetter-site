Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

One Version Better Studio ("the app") is a private tool used solely by the owner of the One Version Better channels to publish their own videos and read their own channel statistics.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth / Instagram login), the app accesses only the owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts: uploading and scheduling the owner's videos, editing their titles and descriptions, and reading aggregate analytics such as views and watch time. It does not access data of any other user.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored privately by the owner and used only to perform the actions above. Analytics are used only to improve the owner's own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Deletion

Stored tokens can be deleted at any time by revoking access as above or by contacting the owner.
